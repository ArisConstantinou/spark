## Development and runtime tooling

Node.js and .NET are installed separately on this PC and are not bundled by this source project. Playwright and Sharp are used only by headless development verification from the existing Codex dependency runtime; they are not runtime browser dependencies. No LibreDWG/GPL component is incorporated.

## CAD verification samples

Autodesk sample DWG files are read in place from the existing AutoCAD installation for local verification only. They remain third-party reference files and are not distributed, copied into the app, or presented as Aris's designs.

## Application visuals

Electrical symbol vectors, interface icons and the welcome-plan illustration are authored in this project's source. They are schematic drawing aids and do not claim conformance to a particular licensed symbol standard. Browser/system fonts are used without bundled font files.
